**Solmar Bay** — an original open-world crime game in the browser. You are Danny Cole,
a courier who owes money to the wrong people. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot
and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay Police Department, and finish three story
missions: a package run, a "borrowed" red Comet, and one last job at the Harbor Pier.

The city is alive: traffic cruises the grid, pedestrians stroll the sidewalks (and flee
when trouble starts), police cruisers chase you as your wanted stars climb, and your
progress — cash, missions, position — saves to local storage.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## How to play

- **WASD** — move on foot / drive · **Mouse** — look around (click to lock the pointer)
- **Shift** — run · **E** — steal / enter a car, **E** again to get out
- **Click** — punch · **F** — shoot the pistol · **Space** — brake while driving
- **P** — pause (save, mute, restart) · Ammo crates around town refill your pistol

Stealing cars, hitting pedestrians and shooting raise your wanted level — up to 5 stars.
Lose the cops by breaking line of sight and staying quiet, or get busted and lose 20%
of your cash. Finish all three missions to complete the story.
