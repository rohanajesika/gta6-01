SOLMAR BAY

An open-world crime story on the sunny coast

Mara Voss came to Solmar Bay with $250 and a borrowed name. Fixer Delia Kross has five jobs — steal, deliver, escape, defend, and one last heist. The city is yours: walk its streets, take any car, and mind the Bay Police.

  • WASD — move / drive
  • Shift — run
  • Mouse drag — look around
  • E — enter / exit a car
  • Click — shoot  ·  F — punch
  • M — start the next job  ·  P — pause  ·  S — save

A wholly original game. No characters, names, art or music from any commercial game.
